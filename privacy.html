Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Summary: We collect minimal data to provide our AI body measurement and virtual try-on services. We do not sell your personal information. Your body scan data is encrypted and stored securely.

1. Introduction

KORRA AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile application, and services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password when you create an account
  • Body Scan Data: Photos uploaded for body measurement analysis, 3D body models, and measurement results
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through Paystack (we do not store card details)
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team or through the platform

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interaction patterns
  • Camera Access: When you use virtual try-on, your camera feed is processed locally and not stored on our servers

2.3 Third-Party Authentication

If you sign in using Snapchat or other third-party services, we receive your basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) as authorized by you.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Services: Generate body measurements, create 3D models, and enable virtual try-on
  • Improve Accuracy: Analyze measurement data to improve our AI models (anonymized and aggregated)
  • Process Payments: Handle subscriptions and one-time purchases
  • Communicate: Send service updates, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

4. Camera and Body Scan Data

Important: Your camera feed for virtual try-on is processed in real-time using Snapchat Camera Kit and is never recorded or stored. Body scan photos are encrypted and stored securely, and can be deleted at any time.

  • Camera Access: We access your camera only when you initiate a virtual try-on session. The feed is processed locally in your browser and transmitted directly to Snapchat's AR engine.
  • Body Scan Photos: Photos uploaded for measurement are stored encrypted on secure servers (Supabase Storage) and are only accessible by you.
  • 3D Models: Generated body models are stored securely and associated with your account.
  • Retention: Body scan data is retained until you delete it or your account is closed.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Supabase (hosting/storage), Paystack (payments), and Snapchat (AR processing) - all bound by strict data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to you)

6. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Body scan photos are stored in isolated storage buckets with access controls
  • We implement regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access to production systems is logged and monitored

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Object: Opt-out of data processing for analytics

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers, including standard contractual clauses where required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
