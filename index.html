Global Reach

Designed for trust, regardless of distance.

KORRA helps tailors and brands serve customers far beyond their physical shop.

This is especially vital for businesses serving customers living abroad or managing remote orders across different cities and borders.

Korra makes remote tailoring reliable by helping customers capture and share accurate body measurements directly from their phone. This gives tailors a consistent way to work with remote clients, reducing rework and improving fit confidence.

A customer in London can share their measurements in minutes, while a tailor in Lagos delivers with perfect accuracy — expanding business opportunities without the limits of location.

How KORRA works

Built for tailors who want speed and individuals who want fit.

1
SCAN
Stand in front of your phone and let our AI-powered mobile tailor guide you through a quick front and side photo process.
2
SAVE
Your measurements are converted into a Digital Twin. This 3D blueprint captures your unique posture and volume, eliminating the 'manual ease' guesswork for your tailor.
3
SHARE
Send your profile to any tailor, brand, or shopping site via our measurement link or QR code.
